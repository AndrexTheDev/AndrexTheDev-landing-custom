About

I’m Andrex. I’m self‑taught, and for years I was better at having ideas than finishing them. My notes are a graveyard of plans — a satirical AI court for crypto drama, a battle‑royale game, a motor that runs on warm water.

In August I changed one rule: nothing counts until it has a URL. Since then I’ve shipped NodeChart and GitBinder, and I’m halfway through a DJ app.

I use AI coding tools a lot, and I’m open about it. But tools don’t ship products — testing does. NodeChart passed 463 release checks before v1.0. GitBinder runs 714 automated tests.

Everything I make is free. No signup walls, no user data to leak. If a tool saves you time, that’s the whole business plan.
